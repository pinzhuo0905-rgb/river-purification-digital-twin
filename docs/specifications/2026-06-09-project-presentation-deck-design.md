# Presentation Deck & Defense Materials - Design Specification

## 1. Presentation Goals
Deliver a 22-slide high-definition visual presentation detailing the research motivation, numerical mechanics, engineering architecture, and live demonstration for academic project defense.

## 2. Visual Standards
* **Design Philosophy**: Apple Liquid Glass aesthetic—translucent surfaces, ambient refraction, water-mist gradients, and precise HUD data meters.
* **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 widescreen HD (1920x1080).
* **Deliverables**:
  * Standalone vector presentation deck (`liquid_glass_river_ppt.pptx`).
  * Verbatim academic speech script (`speech.md`).
  * Single-page research poster (`poster/output/`).
